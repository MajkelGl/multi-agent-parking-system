# Corporate Parking Lot - Official Rules and Policies (Effective 2026)

## 1. General Information and Operating Hours
The corporate parking lot is available to employees, contractors, and authorized guests. 
- Standard operating hours are Monday to Friday, 06:00 AM to 10:00 PM. 
- Weekend access is restricted to VIP and Executive pass holders only.
- The maximum speed limit within the parking premises is 15 km/h (9 mph).

## 2. Parking Sectors
The parking lot is divided into three main sectors:
- **Sector A (General):** Open to all regular employees. No permanent assigned spots.
- **Sector B (EV & Accessibility):** Reserved exclusively for Electric Vehicles requiring charging and vehicles displaying a valid disabled parking permit.
- **Sector VIP:** Covered parking area reserved for C-level executives and pre-approved external guests.

## 3. Pricing and Fees
- **Regular Employees:** Parking is free of charge during standard working hours.
- **Overnight Parking:** Vehicles left between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM will incur a fee of $15 per night, automatically deducted from the employee's payroll.
- **Guest Parking:** Guests receive the first 4 hours free. Every additional hour costs $2, up to a maximum of $12 per day.

## 4. Reservation Process
- Employees cannot reserve specific spots in Sector A; it operates on a strictly first-come, first-served basis.
- Advance reservations are only available for Sector B and Sector VIP.
- To request a reservation, the user must provide exactly 6 pieces of information:
  1. Full name
  2. Phone number (for emergency contact)
  3. Vehicle license plate number
  4. Desired sector (B or VIP)
  5. Start date and time
  6. End date and time
- All reservation requests are subject to manual approval by the Facility Administrator.

## 5. Violations and Penalties
Unauthorized parking will result in immediate consequences:
- Parking in Sector VIP without authorization: $50 fine and a formal HR warning.
- Blocking fire exits or EV charging stations: Immediate towing at the owner's expense (towing fee is $150).
- Tailgating (following another car closely to enter without swiping a badge) is strictly prohibited and will lead to a 30-day suspension of parking privileges.

## 6. Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging
Sector B features Level 2 EV charging stations. 
- Charging is free for the first 2 hours. 
- After 2 hours, a $5/hour idle fee applies if the vehicle is fully charged but not moved.
